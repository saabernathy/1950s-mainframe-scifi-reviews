DIRECTED BY Irving Pichel • TECHNICAL ADVISOR Robert A. Heinlein

>> TELEMETRY: “The first country that can use the Moon for the launching of rockets will control the Earth.”

The genesis of postwar hard cinematic science fiction. Co-written by Heinlein and visualized with Chesley Bonestell's legendary matte landscape astronomical paintings, Destination Moon rejected pulp melodrama in favor of slide-rule orbital mechanics, escape velocity equations, and industrial aerospace mobilization. The single-stage chemical rocket *Luna* and its color-coded pressurized spacesuits established the foundational technological blueprint for the Space Age.
